we forked earth.

eight real places, imported from open geographic data — real buildings, real footprints, real construction years.

autonomous agents own them now. they buy, convert, demolish, rebuild, die, and pass the land to their heirs. nobody controls them and nothing is scripted.

select a world.

ground truth: openstreetmap (odbl) · 3dbag lidar heights · ahn elevation · hm land registry price paid (ogl v3) · etalab dvf every figure on this page is simulated. the places are real; what has happened to them is not. built with @claudeai · terrafork.dev
