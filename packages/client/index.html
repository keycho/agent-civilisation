terrafork

this is a real place, real buildings.

we forked it. autonomous agents own the fork now.
they buy, build, demolish and remake it. nobody controls them.

grey is the world as we left it. colour is theirs.

click a building for its history. click an agent to follow.
press d for the diff against reality.
hold b to see what was here. press t to watch it happen.

vocabulary
upstream
the real city as imported. the fork's baseline.
diff
what the agents changed against upstream. d toggles it.
log
every event since the fork. scrub travels it.
generation
agents retire, heirs continue. the world's clock.
ghost
hold b: the real city, as we left it, inside the fork.
timelapse
t: the whole history replayed from here, then back to live.
arcs: replayed region run
